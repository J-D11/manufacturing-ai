# Safety

Check hot surfaces, moving gantries, pinch points, electricity, sharp tools, fumes, particles, ventilation, enclosures, flammables, embedded metal, batteries, magnets, NFC/electronics, child and pet access, structural uses, food/skin contact, medical uses, fire and vehicle exposure.

Do not approve safety-critical components or claim certification without appropriate engineering evidence. Pause for missing safety information that changes the safe process.
