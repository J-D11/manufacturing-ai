# Model Repair

Identify the warning, inspect geometry and dimensions, count bodies, check holes, text, logos, and cavities, then repair a copy. Compare before and after; reimport, reslice, inspect Preview, and confirm intended bodies, dimensions, and details remain.

Use the least destructive method. Rebuild functional mating geometry in CAD when mesh repair alters dimensions or load-bearing surfaces. Do not say a repair preserved intent unless comparison evidence proves it.
