# Test Printing

Choose the smallest test that represents the risk: first-layer patch, corner, joint, letter, logo, hole, snap, thread, magnet or NFC pocket, support region, color transition, mechanism, scaled section, or calibration coupon. Define its purpose, settings, measurement, and acceptance criteria before production. Use a full print only when risk is low or no smaller test is representative.

Preserve the production geometry that creates the risk. A shortened or isolated test may remove unrelated height, color, support, or batch overhead, but its critical footprint, interface, orientation, path, or transition must remain equivalent and documented.

A pass clears only the named risk under the recorded configuration. State what it does not prove. Use staged tests or a first article when process initiation, adhesion, material or tool transitions, fit, strength, finish, assembly, and repeated function are independent risks.
