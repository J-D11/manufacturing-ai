# Hardware Integration

For NFC tags, magnets, screws, inserts, bearings, electronics, batteries, weights, adhesives, and similar components, capture exact dimensions, tolerance, orientation, retention, access, replacement path, heat exposure, structural effect, and safety risks.

For embedded components, decide between a pause and post-installation. Check toolhead collision, movement, heat, conductivity, bed damage, required securing, metal/NFC interference, NFC read range through the planned wall, and whether the component must be test-scanned or function-tested. Compare weight options by mass, volume, securing method, and center-of-mass impact. Design pockets and retention to prevent rattle while preserving assembly order and serviceability.
