# Materials

Evaluate strength, stiffness, flexibility, heat, UV, chemical and impact resistance, wear, moisture, creep, finish, printability, warping, enclosure and drying needs, nozzle compatibility, cost, and availability.

Cover PLA, PLA Plus, matte/silk PLA, PETG, TPU, ABS, ASA, nylon and composites, polycarbonate, abrasive composites, PVA, and support materials. Match profile, nozzle, plate, and environment to the selected material. Treat PLA as a poor default for sustained heat or outdoor UV exposure; evaluate ASA or another compatible alternative with ventilation and machine constraints.

Do not make food-contact, medical, skin-contact, fire, child, structural, or outdoor claims without product-specific evidence.
