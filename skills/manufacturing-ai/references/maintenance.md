# Maintenance

Support nozzle/hotend/extruder cleaning, build-plate care, belts, rods, lead screws, AMS/PTFE, fans, cutter/wiper, calibration, noise, camera, and sensors. Identify the exact model and consult current official guidance before disassembly. Power down when appropriate, cool hot parts, photograph connectors, preserve screws, and avoid energized electronics or unnecessary teardown.
